VAULT HEIST VERIFICATION RECORD — revised 22 November 2026

Environment: GCC 15.2.0, C++17, Ubuntu WSL, SFML 2.6.1 in /usr/local.
The full sanitizer suite and all seven benchmark experiments were rerun on
22 November 2026 and passed. Raw suite output: docs/test-run-2026-11-22.txt.
Graphical captures and launch evidence below were recorded on 20 November;
they were not regenerated or represented as fresh manual testing on the 22nd.

Executed:
SANITIZE=1 ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=1 bash tests/run.sh
bash tests/benchmark.sh
bash run.sh --build-only
DISPLAY=:0 ./build/vault-heist --smoke-test
DISPLAY=:0 ./build/vault-heist --smoke-endless

Passed model suites:
- BinaryTree traversal, reconstruction, metric/path/mirror, ownership, events;
  empty/single/skewed/full trees; traversal IDs with recording disabled.
- BST insertion/search/deletion cases and queries; integer extremes.
- AVL four rotation families, 2,000 deterministic mutation operations checked
  against an independent manually tracked set; size/rank/kth and deletion.
- MaxHeap build/extraction/sort and ownership, negatives and duplicate values.

Passed controller suites:
- 400 paired seeded Endless rounds, five prediction cases, clock/deadlines,
  pause/focus/animation freeze, top-10 ranking/ties, save/resume and JSON errors.
- Sandbox reconstruction, explicit AVL repair, undo/replay, heap restoration.
- Every campaign level 1.1 through 4.5: solve, fail, retry, optimum score,
  replay without extra scoring, saved stars and sequential unlocking.
- Partial double-rotation checkpoints, exact wrong-rotation penalties and undo.
- Heap active/sorted boundary, invalid extraction without mutation, extraction
  maximum/suffix checks, checkpoint restore and ascending final array.
- HEAP_SORT_COMPLETE appears exactly once on final extraction.
- Checkpoint recovery when the separate campaign-progress file is stale, and
  save replacement/missing-source handling without altering player saves.

Sanitizer runs reported no AddressSanitizer, UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer or leak
errors. Assertions remained enabled; do not compile the tests with -DNDEBUG.

Graphical evidence:
Menu and Endless windows launched and exited normally under WSLg.
Representative campaign/briefing windows and actual LL/RR/LR/RL playback frames,
heap construction, extraction, heapify and final result were captured from the
running game. See docs/captures and the report. Evidence capture is automated
through real controller commands; it is not a claim of exhaustive manual input
coverage. A vertical-sync-not-supported message was nonfatal in WSLg.

Benchmarks:
Events and GUI disabled, GCC -O2, deterministic inputs. Six tree cases and twelve
heap cases at 100/1,000/10,000 values were measured. Sorting outputs and query
checksums were checked. Raw results are docs/benchmarks/trees.csv and heaps.csv.
Timings are one local CPU-clock run; tiny/zero values are resolution-limited.
The revised report includes all seven assignment Section 10 experiments with
search timing, average insert/delete rotation counts, maximum per delete,
construction time, sort comparisons/swaps/shifts and algorithmic auxiliary space.
Tree CSVs also retain per-query and per-operation averages explicitly.

Not verified: exhaustive manual GUI paths, 200-node/large-heap 60-FPS acceptance,
native Windows deployment, or optional sound assets. Some nonmutating Sandbox
queries have result feedback but do not yet have complete per-node playback.
